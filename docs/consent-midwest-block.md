## ND
- Min age tattoo: 18 (N.D. Cent. Code § 12.1-31-13).
- Parental consent: allowed at any age under 18; must be in writing AND the parent/legal guardian must be present at the time of tattooing ("unless in the presence of and with the written consent of a parent or legal guardian").
- Required disclosures (NDAC 33-41-01.1-07, eff. 10/1/2026): consent form must include (1) risk notification — infection, allergic reaction, scarring; (2) client evaluation questionnaire with mandatory language "Consult a physician…" plus 10 listed health questions; (3) aftercare section — department-approved care instructions, advice to seek medical care, shop's name, address, phone number; (4) consent statement confirming the procedure is voluntary, client understands it, had opportunity to ask questions, received/understood aftercare. Artist must also record: artist name, procedure type, body location, design, sterilization/ink lot data, date/time, complications, artist's signature.
- Retention: UNVERIFIED under new NDAC 33-41-01.1 (contents in § 33-41-01.1-06, no explicit period found). Repealed NDAC 33-41-01-06 required 3 years; spore-test records kept 3 years.
- ID: client must provide name and DOB as shown on a government photo ID, and a copy of the client's government photo ID must be retained; for minors, form must also record parent/guardian's name and signature, and a copy of the parent's/guardian's government photo ID must be retained, with the parent physically present (guardianship letters/orders also retained).
- Barber/cosmetology: none found at state level.
- Citations: N.D. Cent. Code § 12.1-31-13; NDAC ch. 33-41-01.1 §§ 33-41-01.1-06, -07 (effective 10/1/2026).

## SD
- Min age tattoo: 18 (SDCL 26-10-19).
- Parental consent: allowed at any age under 18; parents must sign a consent form authorizing the tattoo (SDCL 26-10-19). No minimum age below 18; statute does not require parent present, but some municipal ordinances require parent to sign in presence of an employee. Violation = Class 2 misdemeanor.
- Required disclosures: none at state level. ARSD 44:12:01:13 requires signed consent from every patron and posted notice that no minor may be tattooed without signed parental consent; service prohibited on patrons with communicable skin disease or appearing under influence. Tattooing regulation largely delegated to municipalities (SDCL 9-34-17) — municipal examples (Sioux Falls-area code, Pennington County) require signed consent including disease-freedom statement.
- Retention: UNVERIFIED at state level (Sioux Falls-area ordinance example: 2 years).
- ID: UNVERIFIED at state level; city/county ordinances set their own.
- Barber/cosmetology: none found at state level.
- Citations: SDCL 26-10-19; SDCL 9-34-17; ARSD 44:12:01:13.

## NE
- Min age tattoo: 18 (Neb. Rev. Stat. § 38-10,165).
- Parental consent: allowed at any age under 18. § 38-10,165: "No person shall perform body art on or to any person under eighteen years of age without the prior written consent of the parent or court-appointed guardian of such person. The person giving such consent must be present during the procedure. A copy of such consent shall be retained for a period of five years by the person performing such body art." Violation = Class III misdemeanor. (No notarization in codified text; older "notarized" claim from 2004 LB906 debate materials is superseded.)
- Required disclosures: none mandated in 172 NAC 44 (UNVERIFIED). Rule 002.01 defines "aftercare" as written instructions; 012.01 requires aftercare pamphlets provided/posted along with current regulations, inspection report, each practitioner's license.
- Retention: adults — 3 years; under-18 records and consent forms — 5 years; past-year records kept on-site; confidential (172 NAC 44 § 012.03(C)). § 38-10,165 also requires the minor-consent copy retained 5 years.
- ID: all clients — copy of client's federal or state photo ID showing photo and DOB (driver's license, military ID, state ID, or passport) in the client record; under-18 — additionally copy of parent/guardian's photo ID with DOB, consent form signed by both parent/guardian and client, copy of relationship document (birth certificate, adoption papers, guardianship records, or court documents) (172 NAC 44 § 012.03). Client record also includes name/address/DOB, known allergies, healing-hampering conditions, name of body artist, date, body location, procedure description.
- Barber/cosmetology: none found at state level.
- Citations: Neb. Rev. Stat. § 38-10,165; 172 NAC 44 §§ 002.01, 012.01, 012.03.

## KS
- Min age tattoo: 18 (K.S.A. 65-1953).
- Parental consent: allowed at any age under 18; requires prior written AND notarized consent of parent or court-appointed guardian; consenting person must be present during the procedure; written permission plus guardianship letters retained; violation = class A misdemeanor (K.S.A. 65-1953).
- Required disclosures: K.A.R. 69-15-15(c) — before each service, establishment must give client written preservice information advising of possible reactions, side effects, potential complications of tattooing, plus special instructions for 14 listed conditions: diabetes; allergies; cold sores/fever blisters; epilepsy; heart conditions; hemophilia; hepatitis; HIV/AIDS; blood-thinning medication; moles/freckles at tattoo site; psoriasis/eczema; pregnant/nursing; scarring; any other relevant condition. K.A.R. 69-15-15(d): aftercare instructions verbal + written after every service. Service prohibited on: inebriated/incapacitated persons; persons showing IV-drug-use signs; persons with sunburn, skin disease, open lesions, rashes, wounds; treatment areas with psoriasis/eczema (§ 69-15-15(g)).
- Retention: CONFLICT — flag for counsel: K.S.A. 65-1941, as amended by HB 2125 (2023–24 enrolled bill), changed case-history-card retention from 5 years to 3 years; K.A.R. 69-15-15(b) as published still reads at least 5 years (regulation may not have been updated). K.A.R. 69-15-15(b) client record: name/address, date and duration of each service, type of identification presented, type of services provided.
- ID: client record must state "type of identification presented" (K.A.R. 69-15-15(b)); no specific photo-ID type mandated (UNVERIFIED beyond that).
- Barber/cosmetology: none found at state level.
- Citations: K.S.A. 65-1953; K.S.A. 65-1941 as amended by HB 2125 (2023–24); K.A.R. 69-15-15.

## MN
- Min age tattoo: 18 — outright ban. Minn. Stat. § 146B.07, subd. 2(b): tattooing any individual under 18 is a gross misdemeanor regardless of parental or guardian consent. (Piercing carve-out: ages 16–17 may be pierced only with parent/guardian consent form signed in technician's presence; no piercing under 16; no nipple/genital piercing under 18.)
- Required disclosures: § 146B.07, subd. 3 — informed consent must be signed and dated and must disclose verbatim: "(1) that a tattoo is considered permanent and may only be removed with a surgical procedure and that any effective removal may leave scarring; or (2) that body piercing may leave scarring." Subd. 1(b)–(d): health screening must ask about diabetes; hemophilia; skin diseases, lesions, or sensitivities; epilepsy, seizures, fainting, or narcolepsy; anticoagulant use; other relevant info; technician must not perform if client fails to complete/sign, may decline on health grounds. Subd. 5: verbal + written aftercare; written instructions must advise on difference between normal irritation and infection and to consult a health care professional on any indication of infection.
- Retention: 3 years, available for inspection. Record contents: date; information on the required picture ID showing name, age, and current address; copy of the signed/dated authorization form; description of the procedure; name and license number of the technician; copy of the consent form (subd. 4).
- ID: Subd. 1(a): technician must require proof of age from any client stating 18+ via one of: (1) valid driver's license or state ID with photo and DOB; (2) military ID; (3) passport; (4) resident alien card; (5) tribal ID. Record must document info on the picture ID showing name, age, current address.
- Barber/cosmetology: none found at state level.
- Citations: Minn. Stat. § 146B.07, subds. 1–5 (revisor.mn.gov, current).

## IA
- Min age tattoo: 18 — outright ban. Iowa Code § 135.37(2): "A minor shall not obtain a tattoo and a person shall not provide a tattoo to a minor"; "minor" = unmarried person under 18; serious misdemeanor (mirrored at 641 IAC 22.3(2)).
- Required disclosures: 641 IAC 22.6(10) — artist must provide printed aftercare instructions and instruct client to consult a physician if signs of infection appear. No proof-of-age ID method specified.
- Retention: minimum 3 years, kept confidential, available to department/inspection agency. Records must contain: name, DOB, photocopy of identification, date of procedure, name of tattoo artist, client signature (641 IAC 22.15).
- ID: photocopy of identification retained (641—22.15); no specified photo-ID type found.
- Barber/cosmetology: 481 IAC 940.7(157) requires a licensed esthetician, barber/cosmetologist, or electrologist to obtain a signed client consent form BEFORE any service using a certified laser product, intense pulsed light device, chemical peel, or microdermabrasion. Form must: (1) describe in general terms the nature and purpose of the procedure; (2) list known risks if reasonably determinable; (3) state the client acknowledges the disclosure was made and questions answered; (4) be signed by the client or, if the client lacks legal capacity, by a person with legal authority to consent. (Effective 7/1/24; ARC 7920C.) — ONE OF THE VERY FEW VERIFIED SALON-SERVICE CONSENT MANDATES FOUND NATIONWIDE.
- Citations: Iowa Code § 135.37(2); 641 IAC ch. 22 §§ 22.3(2), 22.6(10), 22.15; 481 IAC 940.7(157).

## MO
- Min age tattoo: 18 (Mo. Rev. Stat. § 324.520(2)).
- Parental consent: allowed at any age under 18. § 324.520(2): no tattooing/branding/piercing a minor without prior written informed consent of parent/legal guardian; parent must execute consent in the presence of the person performing the procedure or an employee/agent of the establishment. Fraudulent misrepresentation as a parent = class B misdemeanor.
- Required disclosures: 20 CSR 2267-5.010 §(3)(A)(2) — before tattooing, practitioner must inform each patron in person and in writing of: (A) the dangers for persons with (1) impaired kidney/liver function, (2) diabetes, (3) jaundice within the 12 months prior, (4) blood-thinner therapy, (5) immunocompromising medication; and (B) verbatim: "that the tattoo body piercing and/or branding should be considered permanent, and can only be removed or repaired with a surgical procedure that may leave permanent scarring and disfigurement." Form states patron was informed in person and in writing and agrees to the procedure, is retained at the establishment, and bears the practitioner's signature attesting review, in-person + written advisement, and date. Licensed tattooists must also retain dye records including the lot number of each pigment for each patron (§ 5.010(1)(B)). 20 CSR 2267-5.040(2): verbal and written aftercare instructions after every procedure.
- Retention: minimum 2 years after completion (multi-visit work: 2 years following completion). Patron record: (A) name, address, telephone, DOB; (B) driver's license number or official picture ID number (under-18: parent's DL/picture ID number); (C) signature (parent for under-18); (D) medical/health form covering controlled-substance meds, HIV/Hep B/other bloodborne diseases, allergies, healing-affecting diseases including diabetes, illegal substances, alcohol use, jaundice within 12 months, blood thinners, immunocompromising meds; (E) signed consent form; plus name, license number, and signature of practitioner and note of any adverse effects (20 CSR 2267-5.020 §§ (1), (7)). Establishment must post conspicuous sign stating no under-18 tattoo/pierce/brand and keep license + 2"×2" photo of each practitioner conspicuously displayed (2267-5.020 §(4); 2267-3.010(F)).
- ID: establishment must verify through proper picture ID that patron is at least 18 (2267-5.020 §(5)); record the patron's DL number or official picture ID number (parent's ID number when patron is under 18).
- Barber/cosmetology: none found in codified law. (A "parental consent" definition for genital-area body waxing on minors appears in proposed Missouri cosmetology bills — e.g., SB 1374 (2012), HB 1434 (2024), HB 52 (2025) — but not in codified RSMo § 329.010 per the 2016 statute text; treat as proposed, not law.)
- Citations: Mo. Rev. Stat. § 324.520(2); 20 CSR 2267-5.010, -5.020, -5.040, -3.010 (sos.mo.gov, current through 6/30/24).

## WI
- Min age tattoo: 18 — outright ban. Wis. Stat. § 948.70: anyone who tattoos or offers to tattoo a child commits a Class D forfeiture; only exception is a physician in professional practice (§ 948.70(3)). (Piercing: none under 16; ages 16–17 only with parent/guardian-signed consent form executed in operator's presence; DHS 173.10(1)(b).)
- Required disclosures: DHS 173.10(1)(a) — no tattoo or piercing without signed informed consent on a department-approved form; (b)(5): conspicuous posted sign stating no person under 18 may be tattooed. DHS 173.15(2): oral and written care instructions after each procedure. No procedure on anyone appearing under influence of alcohol or mind-altering drug or with skin lesions/infections in the area (DHS 173.10(1)(c)).
- Retention: UNVERIFIED — DHS 173.10(2) requires a patron record (name, address, age, consent form, name of practitioner, any adverse effects) but states no retention duration.
- ID: no photo-ID requirement found.
- Barber/cosmetology: none found at state level (Wis. Stat. ch. 454 governs licensure/sanitation; no client-consent rule surfaced).
- Citations: Wis. Stat. § 948.70; Wis. Admin. Code ch. DHS 173 §§ 173.10, 173.15 (ch. 173 still current; text per docs.legis.wi.gov archive).

## IL
- Min age tattoo: 18 — outright ban. 720 ILCS 5/12C-35 "Tattooing the body of a minor": anyone not a licensed doctor may not tattoo or offer to tattoo a person under 18, knowingly or recklessly; owner/employee may not permit under-18 to enter or remain where tattooing is performed unless accompanied by parent/legal guardian. Class A misdemeanor. Narrow exceptions: under-18 tattooing another under-18 away from business premises; tattoo removal for trafficking victims/former gang members at a registered establishment. (Piercing only: 720 ILCS 5/12C-40 — no piercing under 18 without written consent of parent/legal guardian; oral-cavity consent must include the quoted warning: "I understand that the oral piercing of the tongue, lips, cheeks, or any other area of the oral cavity carries serious risk of infection or damage to the mouth and teeth, or both infection and damage to those areas, that could result but is not limited to nerve damage, numbness, and life threatening blood clots.")
- Required disclosures: IDPH educational form (IOCI 0177-10, "What to Know Before Getting a Tattoo," required educational materials per 77 Ill. Adm. Code 797.600(a)(c)) requires discussion of: tattoo permanence ("may only take a few minutes to acquire, it is permanent"); bloodborne-disease risks (hepatitis C, hepatitis B, tetanus, HIV/AIDS via contaminated equipment); skin infections (minor to serious, including antibiotic-resistant; redness, swelling, pus-like drainage); granulomas; scars and keloids; allergic reactions (itchy rash); swelling or burning during MRI; plus BBP training, proficiency, spore-test results; instructions to contact a physician immediately on abnormal itching, irritation, redness, swelling, or fever. Health screening (yes/no) must cover: diabetes; hemophilia; skin diseases/lesions/sensitivities; allergies/adverse reactions to pigments, dyes, or latex; epilepsy/seizures/fainting/narcolepsy; anticoagulants; HIV; hepatitis; other relevant info. Form fields include: establishment name/address/phone; client name + signature; artist name + artist signature; date; copy of client's photo ID if collected. 77 Ill. Adm. Code 797.600(b) also requires aftercare instructions.
- Retention: UNVERIFIED — verbatim 77 Ill. Adm. Code 797 text not retrievable from primary online sources.
- ID: 77 Ill. Adm. Code 797.400(k) — tattooing a person under 18 or failure to verify age by government-issued ID with birth date and photograph is a violation; 797.400(k)(l): client consent not collected or not worded correctly is a violation (per official inspection checklist).
- Barber/cosmetology: none found at state level (68 Ill. Adm. Code 1175.305 covers sanitation only; no client-consent/patch-test rule surfaced).
- Citations: 720 ILCS 5/12C-35; 720 ILCS 5/12C-40; 410 ILCS 54 §§ 15, 25, 35, 80, 85; IDPH form IOCI 0177-10 "What to Know Before Getting a Tattoo" (per 77 Ill. Adm. Code 797.600).

## MI
- Min age tattoo: 18 (MCL 333.13102).
- Parental consent: allowed for unemancipated minors under 18. MCL 333.13102: no tattooing/branding/piercing a minor without prior written informed consent of parent/legal guardian plus proof of authority (minor's birth certificate or legal proof of guardianship); parent/guardian must execute consent in the presence of the licensee or an employee/agent.
- Required disclosures: MDHHS "Requirements for Body Art Facilities" (2007 PA 149 / MCL 333.13101 et seq.; dated 09/02/2009; consent/record substance matches current county plan-review enforcement documents): §10.1 — before starting, each client must receive a copy of the Department-provided Disclosure Statement and Notice for Filing Complaints (advises of risks and possible consequences of body art; how to lodge complaints). §10.2 — verbal + written Department-provided aftercare sheet including: (1) site care instructions; (2) signs and symptoms of infection; (3) recommendation to seek medical attention if the site becomes infected/painful or fever occurs shortly after; (4) blood-donation deferral notice. §10.3 — health questionnaire (diabetes/circulation; hemophilia/excessive bleeding; skin disease/lesions/sensitivities to soaps or disinfectants; allergies/adverse reactions to latex, pigments, dyes, disinfectants, metals; epilepsy/seizures/fainting/narcolepsy; anticoagulants; current pregnancy/breastfeeding; other); §10.4 — clients reporting conditions get written suitability/risk information and physician-consult advice; §10.5 — responses recorded on the consent form, client signs/dates. Caveat: 2025 MDHHS rules update announced by local health departments (effective Sept. 11 — year UNVERIFIED; changes include numbing-product prohibitions and weekly spore testing), but no newer full text was retrievable.
- Retention: minimum 3 years — all client records (§12.3). Record contents (§11.1): date; name/address/age-DOB; contact info (phone/email); signature on the Department-provided consent form documenting receipt of the Disclosure Statement, aftercare sheet, and health questionnaire; description of design and location; name of body art technician; complications; for minors: parent/guardian photo ID (driver's license, passport, or state/government ID with name, picture, DOB) + signed consent form executed in presence of the body art technician or another facility worker. §11.2: a copy of the record goes to the client.
- ID: for minors — parent/guardian photo ID as above; record documents client name/address/age-DOB. No state-level license number for individual artists on the form (individual artists NOT licensed by the state — MDHHS FAQ #5; facility licensing only, PA 375 (2010); some localities license artists).
- Barber/cosmetology: none found at state level.
- Citations: MCL 333.13102; MDHHS "Requirements for Body Art Facilities" §§ 10.1–10.5, 11.1, 11.2, 12.3 (09/02/2009).

## IN
- Min age tattoo: 18 (IC 35-45-21-4).
- Parental consent: allowed at any age under 18. IC 35-45-21-4 "Tattooing or Body Piercing a Minor": the exception requires the parent or legal guardian to be present and give written permission at the time the tattoo is done. Violation = Class A misdemeanor. (Note: IC 35-42-2-7 was repealed and relocated to IC 35-45-21-4.)
- Required disclosures: none mandated in 410 IAC 1-5. 410 IAC 1-5-25(d): operator must display department-prepared/approved written materials explaining universal precautions and patrons' rights, including how to report violations. State rule does not mandate written aftercare content; county ordinances (e.g., Dubois County Ordinance 2012-1; Spencer County policy) require artist and patron to sign that aftercare guidelines were reviewed and a written copy was received by the patron.
- Retention: 2 years. Record contents (410 IAC 1-5-28): (1) name; (2) address; (3) age; (4) date tattooed/pierced; (5) design; (6) location on body; (7) name of tattoo artist or body piercer; (8) jewelry/decoration used.
- ID: no state-level ID-type rule found; county practice requires valid government-issued photo ID (Spencer County: two IDs, one government-issued); parents show valid ID plus a copy of the minor's birth certificate.
- Barber/cosmetology: none found at state level.
- Citations: IC 35-45-21-4; 410 IAC 1-5-28; 410 IAC 1-5-25(d).

## OH
- Min age tattoo: 18 (ORC § 3730.06(A)).
- Parental consent: allowed at any age under 18. ORC § 3730.06(B): parent/guardian/custodian must (1) appear in person at the business at the time the procedure is performed and (2) sign a document provided by the business that explains the manner in which the procedure will be performed and methods for proper care of the affected body area following performance.
- Required disclosures: ORC § 3730.06(B) — for minors, the signed document must explain the procedure method and post-procedure care. ORC § 3730.09(A)(2): operator must maintain written records of the color, manufacturer, and lot number of each pigment used for each tattoo. ORC § 3730.07 criminalizes knowingly showing false name/age/ID information to obtain a procedure for someone under 18, or impersonating a parent/guardian/custodian. (Body-art rules at OAC 3701-9-01 to -09, including client-record contents/retention, not retrievable from primary online sources — UNVERIFIED.)
- Retention: UNVERIFIED at the client-record level. Sterilization-device test records must be kept at least 2 years (§ 3730.09(A)(5)). Enforcement at board-of-health approval level (§ 3730.05).
- ID: no specific photo-ID rule found beyond § 3730.07's false-ID offense; age-verification details live in the OAC rules (UNVERIFIED).
- Barber/cosmetology: no codified client-consent requirement found for chemical services. Tanning has detailed consent rules (ORC § 4713.50-2): 18+ self-consent on board-prescribed form; 16–17 parental consent signed in the facility in the operator's presence, valid 90 days, max 45 sessions; OAC 4713-1-07(D) requires licensees to produce government photo ID on inspection; OAC 4713-8-03(G)/(I) require following manufacturer directions and limit chemical-peel concentration/pH.
- Citations: ORC §§ 3730.05, 3730.06, 3730.07, 3730.09(A)(2), 3730.09(A)(5); ORC § 4713.50-2; OAC 4713-1-07(D); OAC 4713-8-03(G), (I).

Cross-cutting notes: Minor tattoos banned outright in 4 of the 12 (MN, IA, WI, IL) — hard-block minor workflows there. Notarization required only in KS (dropped from NE law despite older debate materials); parent in-person presence required in ND, NE, KS, MO (in performer's/agent's presence), MI (in licensee/agent's presence), IN (present at time of tattoo), OH (appear in person). Written permanence disclosures mandated verbatim in MN ("tattoo is considered permanent and may only be removed with a surgical procedure…") and MO ("…should be considered permanent, and can only be removed or repaired with a surgical procedure that may leave permanent scarring and disfigurement") — quote on forms. Photo-ID retention: NE, ND, MN, IL require retaining a copy of the client's photo ID (NE/IL: copy retained; ND: copy retained for both client and parent; MN: ID info in the record); MO requires recording the DL/official ID number; IA requires a photocopy of identification; IN/OH rely on county/board-of-health practice. Artist identity on form/record: ND, NE, MN, IA, WI, IL, MI, IN require artist's name; MN and MO additionally require license number; MO requires practitioner's signature attesting advisement; IL's IDPH form requires artist name + signature and establishment name/address/phone. Biggest gaps: IL (77 Ill. Adm. Code 797 verbatim incl. retention), OH (OAC 3701-9 contents/retention), WI (retention duration), ND (retention under new NDAC 33-41-01.1), KS 3-yr vs 5-yr retention conflict, 2025 MI MDHHS rules update (announced by local HDs, full text not publicly retrievable).
