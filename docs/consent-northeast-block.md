# NORTHEAST block — research received 2026-10-02 (ME, NH, VT, MA, RI, CT, NJ, PA, DE, MD, DC)
Framing: legal information, NOT legal advice.

## ME
- Min age tattoo: 18, no minors even with parent. Parental consent: not allowed for tattoos (32 M.R.S. § 4203). Piercing: prior written parent/guardian consent, parent's ID noted on record (32 M.R.S. § 4323; 10-144 C.M.R. ch. 209 § 4(B)).
- 32 M.R.S. § 4203: "No person shall place a tattoo mark or figure upon a person under the age of 18 years." Violation: civil, $500–$1,000 per violation (32 M.R.S. § 4204).
- Disclosures: medical history obtained; clients with diabetes or severe skin infections advised of added complications; "All clients shall be advised of possible complications from being tattooed"; every client questioned about latex allergy; written aftercare given out loud and in writing, signed copy kept — "Permanent records for each patron, including a signed copy of the written aftercare instructions provided to the patron." No tattooing anyone obviously intoxicated. (10-144 C.M.R. ch. 210 §§ 4(W), 6(B), 4(A))
- Retention: patron records (date; name and address; age; picture ID used + serial number; patron's own signature; signed copy of written aftercare) kept at shop, min 2 years, available on request. Patron personally writes down the picture ID + serial number; practitioner verifies. On sale/closing, records go to the Dept. (10-144 C.M.R. ch. 210 § 6(B), (C))
- ID: required — picture ID with serial number recorded; acceptable: driver's license, liquor ID card, military ID, or other adequate record.
- Barber/cosmetology: no state-mandated consent-form elements found this pass (32 M.R.S. ch. 126; consent provisions UNVERIFIED).

## NH
- Min age tattoo: 18, no minors even with parent. Parental consent: not for tattoos. Piercing only: parent physically present + evidence of parent/guardian status + signed declaration naming piercing location (RSA 314-A:8, III).
- RSA 314-A:8, I prohibits tattooing/branding anyone under 18 (no parental route).
- Disclosures: "Each licensee shall disclose to each client the health risks and potential adverse effects of the procedure prior to beginning any procedure" (Plc 606.04(a) — not specified in writing); written AND oral aftercare to every client (Plc 606.04(b)); for minor piercing, risks disclosed to parent/guardian (RSA 314-A:8, III). No written consent form required for adult tattoos; no health-history form required.
- Retention: all records min 7 years (RSA 314-A:6, I; Plc 606.03(c)(2)). Minor piercing paperwork: 7 years or 1 year past minor's 18th birthday, whichever longer (Plc 606.03(e)).
- ID: licensees keep "a photocopy, photograph, or scan of the identification provided" — valid government-issued ID (Plc 606.03(c)(2)). Client who refuses signs acknowledgment that ID was checked (Plc 606.03(d)).
- Barber/cosmetology: no state-mandated consent-form elements verified (OPLC Plc 600 series cover body art; barber/cosmetology provisions UNVERIFIED). Towns may layer local ordinances (Salem, Laconia, Hudson).

## VT
- Min age tattoo: 18 to consent alone; no floor age — minor of any age may be tattooed with proper parental consent.
- Parental consent: allowed. Written consent of parent/guardian signed in the tattooist's presence; contains parent's certification that info is true and accurate "under pains and penalties of perjury"; tattooist must disclose required info to parent before parent signs. Minor, tattooist, and parent each sign (OPR Minor Tattooing Consent Form). (Admin. Rules for Tattooing and Body Piercing § 3.6; 26 V.S.A. § 4102). Violation = unprofessional conduct (26 V.S.A. § 4108, as amended by 2024 Act 158).
- Piercing: no state age rule — piercers use sound judgment.
- Disclosures: consent form must include: (1) explanation of procedure; (2) permanence; (3) removal may not be complete, may scar; (4) complications — bruising, scarring, bleeding, skin or blood infection, allergic reactions to inks or jewelry, nerve and tissue damage (+ oral-piercing risks); (5) mandated questions: alcohol/drugs in last 8 hours; ate within last 2 hours; heart valve conditions, herpes, latex/antibiotic allergies, diabetes, hemophilia/bleeding disorders, blood thinners, autoimmune disorders, pregnancy; (6) client signature and date. (Code Vt. R. 04-030-310 § 3.2(F)(1)–(2)). Written AND verbal aftercare: wound care, what healing looks like, activity limits, infection signs (§ 3.3(E)). § 4.1 disclosures made by posted notice with separate acknowledgment document.
- Retention: client file (name, address, phone, procedure date, medical conditions disclosed, procedure description, practitioner's name) kept min 2 years from date of first treatment, available to Director on inspection (§ 3.2(E)(9)). Electronic files allowed with client signature (OPR FAQs). On sale/closing, records go to Director.
- ID: no state-mandated client photo-ID rule found for tattoos (UNVERIFIED). Parent identity via in-person signed certification under penalties of perjury.
- Barber/cosmetology: no state-mandated consent-form elements verified (26 V.S.A. ch. 29; details UNVERIFIED).

## MA
- Min age tattoo: 18 — no minors, even with parent (per DPH Model Regulations for Body Art Establishments § 4(A), as locally adopted — e.g., Haverhill; Boston Body Art Regulation § 5.8.1).
- Parental consent: not for tattoos. Piercing (non-genital): parent/legal custodial parent/legal guardian present, properly identified, signs consent form (Model § 4(A)–(B)); genital piercing banned under 18 regardless.
- No statewide statute; local boards of health regulate under MGL ch. 111, § 31. State-level form requirements: none — local rules control.
- Disclosures: (a) artist informs client verbally and in writing that these conditions may increase risks: diabetes, hemophilia, skin disease, pigment allergies, epilepsy, blood thinners, hepatitis, HIV, latex sensitivity; (b) client signs form confirming info provided, no disqualifying condition, consents, received written aftercare (Model §§ 6(D)(1)–(2)); (c) verbal and written aftercare: cleaning, when to call a doctor, shop address and phone (Model § 6(K)).
- Retention: client records (name, DOB, address, procedure date, practitioner name, procedure description/body location, signed consent; under-18: parent ID, presence, consent) kept secure min 3 years, available to Board on request (Model § 5(E)).
- ID: Model requires copy of parent's photo ID for under-18 clients; Boston requires client's own valid proof of identification on consent form. No state rule; varies by board.
- Barber/cosmetology: no state-mandated patch test, chemical-service disclosure, or minor-consent element verified under 240 CMR.

## RI
- Min age tattoo: 18 — no minors, except medical purposes only.
- Parental consent: only via narrow medical exception — ALL required: parent/guardian present; gov-issued photo ID for BOTH minor and parent; written notarized parental consent on department's form; proof of parentage/guardianship; notarized physician (licensed under R.I. Gen. Laws ch. 5-37) consent; work by licensed artist. (R.I. Gen. Laws § 11-9-15(b)). Piercing: parent/guardian present + written consent + parent's proper photo ID (§ 23-1-39(c); 216-RICR-40-10-14 §§ 14.4.5(E)(1), 14.5.8(A)(4)).
- "Every person who shall tattoo any minor under the age of eighteen (18)" = misdemeanor, up to 1 yr and/or $300 (§ 11-9-15(a)).
- Disclosures: no written consent or health-history questions mandated by rule (artist must visually examine area for sores, lesions, wounds, skin disease, signs of drug use). Aftercare mandatory verbally and in writing, instructing patron to consult physician at first sign of infection (216-RICR-40-10-15 § 15.4.5(A)(3)(n)). Apprentice patrons informed in writing, acknowledged via dated signature (§ 15.4.3(B)(4)).
- Retention: patron record (date tattooed; name, address, phone, age; copy of photo ID; tattoo location/design; artist's name) entered in bound book with pre-numbered pages, kept min 5 years after date tattooed (216-RICR-40-10-15 § 15.5.7).
- ID: required — "Photo identification as proof of age (e.g., driver's license, military identification), a copy of which shall be maintained for each patron" (§ 15.5.7(A)(2)(a)). Affirmative defense to § 11-9-15: inspected plausible gov photo ID showing 18+, photocopy attached to info card (§ 11-9-15(c)).
- Barber/cosmetology: no state-mandated consent-form elements verified (216-RICR-40-05-4 cover licensing; consent provisions UNVERIFIED).

## CT
- Min age tattoo: 18 to consent alone; no floor age — unemancipated minor under 18 needs parent/guardian permission.
- Parental consent: allowed. Statute does NOT require permission to be written, notarized, in-person, or parent present (Conn. Gen. Stat. § 20-266p(6)). Piercing: written permission of the minor's PARENT required (guardian not named; presence not required) (§ 19a-92g(a)).
- Tattooing unemancipated minor under 18 without parent/guardian permission = class D misdemeanor (§ 20-266p(6)).
- Disclosures: none at state level — no disclosure, health-history, or aftercare mandates (town ordinances may impose).
- Retention: no state retention period or client-record contents list — set locally by town/district health departments.
- ID: no state ID rule. Photo-ID check is practical way to establish 18.
- Barber/cosmetology: no state-mandated consent-form elements verified (CT DPH technician licensing §§ 20-266o et seq.; barber/cosmetology provisions UNVERIFIED).

## NJ
- Min age tattoo: 18 to consent alone; no floor age — under 18 allowed with written parental consent.
- Parental consent: allowed. Written consent of parent/legal guardian required; parent/guardian must accompany client during procedure; gov-issued photo ID of client AND parent photocopied and kept with application (N.J.A.C. 8:27-4.2(c)). Tattooing minor without written parental permission = disorderly persons offense (N.J.S.A. 2C:40-21). (Emancipated-minor carve-out in rule conflicts with statute — rely on statute.)
- Disclosures: (a) consent form "reviewed verbally with the client prior to performing any procedure(s)," minimally covering: type of procedure(s), its risks, alternatives, generally accepted results, an aftercare plan, and client's signed acceptance of the procedure by the named artist (N.J.A.C. 8:27-4.2(b)3); (b) medical health history form — minimum diabetes, allergies, skin conditions, list of medications (§ 4.2(b)2); (c) signed copy of written aftercare instructions kept on file: site care, possible side effects, restrictions, infection signs, when to see a physician (§ 8:27-7.5(a)).
- Retention: all client records (name, DOB, proof of age, address; emergency contact; procedure date; artist name; procedure type/location; client signature; minors: proof of parental presence + written consent) min 3 years, available to health official on request (N.J.A.C. 8:27-4.2(a), (b)1).
- ID: required — "Government issued photographic identification of the client shall be photocopied and maintained on file with the client's application" and "Identification of the parent or legal guardian shall be photocopied" (N.J.A.C. 8:27-4.2(c)).
- Barber/cosmetology: no client consent elements found — NJ Board of Cosmetology and Hairstyling (N.J.A.C. 13:28) verified only for shop/practitioner records (shop keeps practitioner records 2 years, N.J.A.C. 13:28-3.3(d)); no patch-test or chemical-service consent found.

## PA
- Min age tattoo: 18 to consent alone; no floor age statewide — under 18 allowed with parent/guardian consent AND presence.
- Parental consent: allowed. Parent/guardian must give consent AND be present at time of tattooing — both required; not required in writing. (18 Pa.C.S. § 6311(a)). Piercing for compensation: same consent-and-presence requirement (§ 6311(b)).
- Philadelphia: hard floor of 16 — under 16 only with physician's statement.
- Disclosures: none at state level — no state licensing, no state consent-form content. Local only (e.g., Philadelphia DPH § 8.A(1): verbal and written aftercare before procedure begins, naming the shop, instructing client to see doctor at first sign of infection; Philadelphia advises physician consult for risk categories — skin disease, pigment allergies, anticoagulants, hemophilia, diabetes/immune deficiency, keloids).
- Retention: no statewide retention period — local (Philadelphia and Erie County: 2 years; Easton City Code ch. 526: 2 years).
- ID: no statewide ID rule. Philadelphia: operator must see parent/guardian's photo ID before tattooing a minor; Easton: clients 18 with photo ID; notarized minor consent.
- Barber/cosmetology: no state-mandated consent-form elements verified (state does not license tattoo shops at all; barber/cosmetology provisions UNVERIFIED).

## DE
- Min age tattoo: 18 to consent alone; no floor age — minor (under 18, unemancipated) may be tattooed with proper parental consent.
- Parental consent: allowed. Prior written consent of the minor's parent (over 18) or legal guardian to the specific act of tattooing/branding/piercing — and the consent form must be notarized (11 Del. C. § 1114(a), (c)). Record must also show proof the parent/guardian was present and consented (16 Del. Admin. Code 4451 § 6.3). First offense: class B misdemeanor (plus civil liability of actual damages or $1,000, whichever greater); second/subsequent: class A.
- Disclosures: (a) health questions must cover: diabetes; high blood pressure/heart condition; hemophilia; skin diseases/lesions/sensitivities; allergies or adverse reactions to pigments, dyes, latex; epilepsy/seizures/fainting/narcolepsy; blood thinners or NSAIDs in last 24 hours; medication slowing healing; communicable disease; pregnancy in last 3 months; under influence of drugs/alcohol (4451 § 6.2.1); (b) client signs Release Form confirming the questions were asked (§ 6.2.2); (c) Division-approved educational information and aftercare given orally and in writing, aftercare when work is done, signed and dated by client (§ 6.1.1); (d) no procedures on intoxicated persons (§ 1114(b)).
- Retention: client records (name, DOB, address; copy of state/federally issued photo ID with birthdate; procedure date; operator/technician name; type/location; client signature; minors: proof of parental presence/consent) min 3 years from date of service, available to Division on request; confidential — Division and establishment both bound (4451 §§ 6.2.3, 6.3).
- ID: required — copy of client's state or federally issued photo ID with birthdate kept in record (driver's license, state ID, passport, immigration card) (§ 6.3); statute attaches photocopy to info card the client completes (11 Del. C. § 1114(d)(2)).
- Barber/cosmetology: no state-mandated consent-form elements verified (24 Del. C. ch. 51; details UNVERIFIED).

## MD
- Min age tattoo: none statewide — no statewide minimum age; written consent governs. (Local: Baltimore City bans tattooing anyone under 18 outright — Health Code Title 13; Worcester County: no tattooing under 18; Allegany County: no tattooing/piercing under 18 without parent present + written consent.)
- Parental consent: allowed statewide. Artist must "obtain the client's written consent for the performance of the procedure or, in the case of a minor, the consent of the parent or guardian" (COMAR 10.06.01.06H(1)(a)). No age floor, no notary, no presence, no ID named in state rule.
- Disclosures: (a) disclose the risks of the procedure and obtain client's written consent (parent's/guardian's for minor) (COMAR 10.06.01.06H(1)(a)); (b) mandatory posted notice naming the risks — "pain, bleeding, swelling, infection at the site of the procedure, transmission of blood-borne infections, scarring, and nerve damage" — plus technician duties (obtain written consent, wash hands, cleanse skin, sterile instruments, proper technique, written healing/care instructions) (H(1)(m)); (c) written after-care instructions to every client (H(1)(k)); (d) no work on rash/infected skin, no artist with infected/bleeding hand lesions, no styptic pencils/alum blocks, no reusing single-use instruments (H(2)).
- Retention: signed consent kept 3 years; client records (customer name, date and type of procedure, technician) kept 3 years — both available to health officer on request (COMAR 10.06.01.06H(1)(a), (b)).
- ID: no state photo-ID rule (local ordinance may impose one).
- Barber/cosmetology: no state-mandated consent-form elements verified (COMAR 09.22 covers cosmetology; consent provisions UNVERIFIED).

## DC
- Min age tattoo: 18 to consent alone; 16–17 with written parental consent; no tattoo at all under 16.
- Parental consent: allowed, two tiers. "No body artist shall perform body art procedures on a person under 18 years of age; except, that, if a parent or legal guardian has provided their written consent" and "Tattooing services are permitted for persons 16 years of age and older." (D.C. Code § 47-2853.76e(b)). Parent presence NOT required. Ear piercing at any age with written parental consent; all other piercings banned under 18, no consent route. CAUTION: DC Health's 2017 rules (25-G DCMR § 708.2) still say 18 for all body art; the 2024 statute (D.C. Law 25-191) liberalized it. Confirm with DC Health before relying on statute alone.
- Violations: misdemeanor, fine up to $2,500, up to 3 months, or both (§ 47-2853.76e).
- Disclosures: (a) verbal and written aftercare on completion; written version must tell customer to see a doctor if something goes wrong and carry shop's contact details (D.C. Code § 47-2853.76d); (b) before work, remind each customer to see their doctor about any medical condition body art could worsen; (c) tattoo shops must post the FDA warning about unapproved tattoo pigments and keep pigment records (contents, maker/supplier, recalls), shown to customer who asks; (d) no procedure on customer impaired by illness, alcohol, or drugs (25-G DCMR § 708.4). No written consent form content mandated beyond the minor's parental consent.
- Retention: "The operator shall maintain all records at the establishment for at least three (3) years" (25-G DCMR § 315.1). Electronic records must be backed up and printable (§§ 314.3, 314.4). Shops without their own sterilizer keep a procedure log (artist, customer, date) (§ 312.1(c)).
- ID: no document named — operator must "ensure every customer's age" (25-G DCMR § 201.1). No statutory good-faith defense for misjudging age. Ear-piercing guns: parent/guardian accompanies and hands in signed Parental/Legal Guardian Authorization Form.
- Barber/cosmetology: no state-mandated consent-form elements verified (body artists licensed through Board of Barber and Cosmetology; DCMR Title 25 Subtitle G covers body art health rules).
