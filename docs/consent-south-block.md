# SOUTH block — research received 2026-10-02 (VA, WV, KY, TN, SC, AL, MS, AR, LA, OK)
Framing: legal information, NOT legal advice.

CRITICAL FLAGS:
1. WV law CHANGED — older sources (and WV's own 2011 bill text) describe under-16 ban + in-person consent + parent photo ID. Current text of W. Va. Code §16-38-3 (verified at code.wvlegislature.gov, 2026) contains NONE of those — the 2020 reenactment (HB 4161) rewrote §16-38-3. Current rule: any minor (under 18) needs prior written consent from one parent/guardian. Use current text.

## VA
- Min age tattoo: 18. Va. Code §18.2-371.3 — no tattoo on anyone under 18 except (i) in the presence of the parent/guardian, or (ii) by/under supervision of licensed medical personnel.
- Parental consent: yes — only as the §18.2-371.3 exception (parent/guardian present). 18VAC41-50-410(E): client and parent/guardian "if applicable" receive disclosures verbally and in writing on the board-prescribed form, with both signatures required.
- Required disclosures: 18VAC41-50-410(E) — before tattooing, each client (and parent/guardian if applicable) must be informed verbally AND in writing, using the client disclosure form prescribed by the board (Board Form A450-12TDIS), with signatures of both client and tattooer acknowledging receipt. Board-prescribed form content (quoted): the procedure is invasive and penetrates the skin; risk of infection including bloodborne pathogens (HIV, hepatitis B/C); allergic reactions to dyes, pigments, or metals; pain, and the artist cannot legally provide anesthesia; risks list of pain, infection, allergic reaction, permanence of the tattoo, and risks of removal.
- Retention: 18VAC41-50-410(F) — records kept at the parlor at least 2 years following the date of the last entry. Record contents: (1) client name, address, phone; (2) date performed; (3) client's age, DOB, and copy of the positive ID; (4) ink colors used and, when available, manufacturer's catalogue/ID number of each color; (5) body location; (6) name of the tattooer; (7) statement that client received a copy of applicable written care instructions and has read and understands them; (8) client signature (+ parent/guardian if applicable).
- ID: 18VAC41-50-410(A)–(B) — client must present valid government-issued positive ID containing photograph and printed DOB (driver's license, passport, or military ID); tattooer must verify and document age, DOB, and ID type in the permanent client record. No alcohol/drugs intoxication allowed (410(C)).
- Barber/cosmetology: none found.
- Citations: Va. Code §18.2-371.3; 18VAC41-50-410 (Regulations booklet, DPOR, updated 8-1-2025).

## WV
- Min age tattoo: 18 default ("minor" = under 18, §16-38-1). Current §16-38-3 contains no under-16 floor.
- Parental consent: yes — §16-38-3(b)(1): "Prior written consent for tattooing of minors shall be obtained from one parent or guardian." Current text requires no in-person execution and no parent photo ID.
- Required disclosures: §16-38-3(a)(4) — before tattooing, owner or artist must discuss with patron the risks involved, including the potential that a tattoo may interfere with the clinical reading of a magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) study (WV is the only state found so far with an MRI-interference disclosure); owner must provide written information regarding possible complications, prepared by the Dept. of Health; receipt must be acknowledged in writing by the patron and kept in the patron's record. §16-38-3(c)(1)–(2): printed instructions on care of the skin after tattooing given to each patron to prevent infection, plus a copy posted in a conspicuous place clearly visible to the person being tattooed.
- Retention: §16-38-3(a)(5) — 5 years for all records, kept by the studio registration holder. §16-38-3(b)(2): written minor consents kept 5 years. Record contents per (a)(2): patron's name and signature, address, age, date tattooed, design, body location, name of the tattoo artist. Sobriety attestation required (b)(3).
- ID: no photo-ID requirement found in §16-38-3 (age is recorded in the record). UNVERIFIED.
- Barber/cosmetology: none found.
- Citations: W. Va. Code §§16-38-1, 16-38-3 (current text via code.wvlegislature.gov; 2020 HB 4161 reenactment).

## KY
- Min age tattoo: 16. 902 KAR 45:065; KRS 211.760.
- Parental consent: yes — ages 16–17 only, with written NOTARIZED consent containing: parent/guardian printed name, government-issued photo ID number, address, phone; minor's printed name and DOB (+ minor's photo ID number if applicable); consent statement; signatures of parent/guardian and minor; signature date; notary seal/attestation.
- Required disclosures: 902 KAR 45:065 §8(1) — before tattooing, written information on: risk of infection; permanence of the tattoo; removal may leave scars. §8(2) — aftercare instructions (written, verbal, or electronic) covering: care of the tattooed site; possible side effects; restrictions; signs/symptoms of infection; instruction to consult a physician if signs appear (fever, excessive swelling/redness, drainage).
- Retention: 902 KAR 45:065 §8(4) — client records kept 2 years. Contents per §8(3): studio name + certificate number; date; client name, DOB, address, phone; copy of client's government photo ID (if applicable) or parent/guardian's; artist name; type/location/description of tattoo; client attestations (not intoxicated, not pregnant, no anticoagulant in prior 24 hrs).
- ID: 902 KAR 45:065 §8(3) — copy of client's government-issued photo ID kept with the record; minor's parent/guardian's ID copied for the notarized consent.
- Barber/cosmetology: none found.
- Citations: KRS 211.760; 902 KAR 45:065.

## TN
- Min age tattoo: 18. Tenn. Code Ann. §62-38-207 (as amended by Public Chapter 981).
- Parental consent: yes, narrowly — ages 16–17 only, and ONLY to cover up an existing tattoo: written parent/guardian consent plus proof of guardianship/custody, parent/guardian present, and acknowledgment of receipt of the §62-38-207(b)(2) report. Minor who lies about age commits a delinquent act ($50–$250 fine + ≥20 hrs community service).
- Required disclosures: Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 1200-23-3-.04(4)(f) — after each tattooing, written instructions given to each patron on care of the tattooed skin to prevent infection, plus a copy posted in a conspicuous place clearly visible to the person being tattooed. Instructions must cover, at minimum: bandaging, cleaning, sun exposure, scratching, wearing of clothing over or around the tattoo site.
- Retention: 1200-23-3-.04(4)(e) — records kept on establishment premises minimum 2 years: date of visit, printed name, signature, address, age, design, body location, tattooist's name.
- ID: no photo-ID requirement found in statute or rules. UNVERIFIED.
- Barber/cosmetology: none found.
- Citations: TCA §62-38-207; Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 1200-23-3-.04(4)(e)–(f).

## SC
- Min age tattoo: 18 — outright minor ban. S.C. Code §44-34-100 — unlawful to tattoo anyone under 18 (minor and parent not liable; artist faces misdemeanor, ≤$2,500 fine and/or 1 yr imprisonment).
- Parental consent: not allowed — cannot legalize tattooing a minor in SC.
- Required disclosures: S.C. Code §44-34-60 — signed informed consent/release form must include notice that tattooing disqualifies the client from donating blood and at a minimum, aftercare suggestions for the specific tattoo site. Reg. 61-111 §1001 — client informed of risks, adverse effects, and consequences; voluntary written choice; blood-donor-disqualification notice. §900.G — artist must ask about conditions affecting healing, including allergies to tattoo dyes/inks or latex and anticoagulant medications. §900.I — aftercare recommendations = care instructions, possible side effects, restrictions, infection-control information. §1003 — procedures and charges in writing, signed before the procedure.
- Retention: Reg. 61-111 §802.C — client records kept ≥6 years following client release. Record contents per §801: photo-ID copy/verification; signed informed consent per §1001; procedure and site; adverse-event/emergency procedures; physician statement if applicable; emergency contact; sobriety attestation; release/aftercare note.
- ID: S.C. Code §44-34-60 and Reg. 61-111 §900.B — artist must verify age by picture ID (valid driver's license or official photographic ID); no tattoo on anyone under 18. (Artist license number on the form: UNVERIFIED — not required by these sections.)
- Barber/cosmetology: none found.
- Citations: S.C. Code §§44-34-100, 44-34-60; DPH Reg. 61-111 §§801, 802.C, 900.B/G/I, 1001, 1003.

## AL
- Min age tattoo: no statutory floor age; minors allowed with consent. Ala. Code §§22-17A-2, 22-17A-3.
- Parental consent: yes — 420-3-23-.15(1)(a): body art on a minor requires informed written consent of parent/legal guardian, given in person to the operator at the time tattooing commences.
- Required disclosures: Ala. Admin. Code 420-3-23 (Disclosure Statement provisions; exact subsection number UNVERIFIED) — Disclosure Statement with bold-type wording: "There may be risks associated with the procedures of commercial body art which includes permanent tattoos, body piercing and permanent cosmetic application, that may adversely affect the healing process, or may be a concern due to potential disease transmission, if you have, or have had, any of the following conditions:" followed by 14-item list — Hepatitis B/C history; HIV/AIDS; diabetes; hemophilia or other blood disorders; skin diseases or lesions; sensitivities to soaps/disinfectants; allergies or adverse reactions to pigments, dyes, latex; tuberculosis; immune disorders; epilepsy/seizures/fainting/narcolepsy; scarring (keloids); heart murmur or heart disease; anticoagulant medications; pregnancy or breast feeding/nursing. ADPH requires the statement posted for public view (TattooDisclosureStatement.pdf). Site-care information (Appendix D): written information sheet with general site-care instructions given to each client by the operator upon completion of the procedure, bearing the facility's name, address, and phone number, and advising the client to consult a physician at first sign of infection (redness, swelling, drainage, prolonged pain, or fever).
- Retention: 420-3-23-.07(2) — client records, release forms, and all client-signed materials retained permanently (prior 3 years on premises; older records may be kept offsite in-county, readily accessible). Record contents per .07(1): client name, DOB, address; procedure date; operator name; type and locations of procedures; client signature (+ minor's parental-consent signature). Records kept confidential.
- ID: no photo-ID rule found in these sections. UNVERIFIED.
- Barber/cosmetology: none found.
- Citations: Ala. Code §§22-17A-2, 22-17A-3; Ala. Admin. Code 420-3-23-.07, -.15; ADPH Tattoo Disclosure Statement.

## MS
- Min age tattoo: 18 — outright minor ban. MSDH Regs., Title 15, Part 19, Subpart 60, Ch. 11, Rule 11.1.3: "It shall be unlawful for any person to perform tattooing and/or body piercing upon any person under the age of eighteen (18) years." (Miss. Code §§73-61-1, 73-61-3.)
- Parental consent: not allowed — no exception in the rule.
- Required disclosures: Rule 11.5.10(1)(k)–(l) — consent form must contain: acknowledgment of the risks involved in tattooing, and acknowledgment of receipt of the printed aftercare responsibilities of the client. Rule 11.5.10(2) — consent form must contain a recommendation that any tattooing or piercing involving the mucous membranes (e.g., tongue or genitalia) be performed by a licensed physician or oral surgeon. Rule 11.5.10(3) — warning to persons who are diabetic, HIV infected, or have End Stage Renal Disease that they are at higher risk of complications and should consult their personal physician first. Rule 11.5.10(4) — warning to patrons with preexisting cardiac conditions (especially with mucous-membrane piercing) that bacteria in the bloodstream can further damage the heart, and they should seek physician permission first. Rule 11.5.11 — every client, before the tattoo procedure begins, must be given a printed statement of aftercare instructions covering at minimum: care and cleaning of the specific area tattooed; signs and symptoms of infections; instructions to consult a medical professional if signs/symptoms of infection appear.
- Retention: Rule 11.5.10 — consent forms maintained at the establishment minimum 2 years, available to Dept. on request. Contents: (a) tattooist's name and Department Certificate of Registration number; (b) supervisor name if provisional; (c) establishment name and address; (d) date; (e)–(i) client name, address, phone, sex, DOB; (j) body location/description; (k) risk acknowledgment; (l) aftercare-receipt acknowledgment; (m) sobriety statement (not under influence of drugs/alcohol); (n)–(p) signatures of client, tattooist, supervisor if required. Photocopy of government-issued ID containing DOB must be attached to the consent form.
- ID: Rule 11.5.10 — photocopy of original government-issued ID containing the client's DOB attached to each consent form.
- Barber/cosmetology: none found.
- Citations: Miss. Code Ann. §§73-61-1, 73-61-3; MSDH Title 15, Part 19, Subpart 60, Ch. 11, Rules 11.1.3, 11.5.10, 11.5.11, 11.5.13 (follow-up complaint log).

## AR
- Min age tattoo: 18 default; §20-27-1502(a) — under 18 requires written consent + parent present + parent shows photo-bearing ID and attests in writing to being the parent/guardian. Violation = Class C misdemeanor. (Separate rule: permanent cosmetics prohibited under age 13, DOH Rules §10.3.)
- Parental consent: yes — under 18: written consent on Department-approved forms, parent/legal guardian physically present, parent signs (signature + printed legal name). DOH Rules §10.3.
- Required disclosures: DOH Rules for Cosmetology and Body Art, Client Information §(h) — artist must discuss risks and complications; advise that certain procedures are permanent and removable only by surgical procedure which may leave scarring; a written cautionary notice to that effect must be furnished to and signed by the client and retained on file. §(i) — oral and written Department-approved aftercare instructions given after the procedure; copy posted in the studio. §(j) — patch test administered upon client request or before any permanent cosmetic procedure.
- Retention: DOH Rules §10.1 — client records preserved at least 1 year from the date of the last entry. However, 2023 statutory amendment (SB 392) requires ≥2 years for photo-ID and proof-of-guardianship copies and permanent-cosmetics records — flag discrepancy; build to 2 years. Record contents per §10.1: date; client name, address, DOB; military branch/rate/rank if applicable; design and location; artist name AND license number; client signature + printed legal name; photocopy of valid government-issued photo ID for each client (minors: guardian's photo ID + proof of guardianship per §10.3); separate bound book per body-art type.
- ID: §20-27-1502(a) — parent/guardian must produce photo-bearing ID and attest in writing to parent/guardian status; §10.1.7 — photocopy of valid government-issued photo ID for each client kept in the record (guardian's ID + proof of guardianship for minors).
- Barber/cosmetology: patch-test rule above is the only verified consent-adjacent item; otherwise none found.
- Citations: Ark. Code §§20-27-1501–1512 (§20-27-1502(a)); AR Dept. of Health Rules for Cosmetology and Body Art §§10.1, 10.3, Client Info (h)–(j).

## LA
- Min age tattoo: 18. La. R.S. 14:93.2 — no tattoo under 18 unless parent/tutor is present and gives consent. Violation: $100–$500 fine and/or ≤1 yr imprisonment.
- Parental consent: yes — under 18: parent or legal custodian/guardian physically present, giving consent, with proper identification per R.S. 14:93.2(A)–(B). LAC tit. 51, §XXVIII-107(H)(5).
- Minor ban?: no — but operators are never required to tattoo minors even with consent (§107(H)(5), §107(M)).
- Required disclosures: §107(A) — consent form completed and signed by each client before the procedure; the form must include a prominent statement regarding the regulatory status of the inks (i.e., whether those inks are regulated or approved by the state of Louisiana or the federal Food and Drug Administration). Aftercare instructions given to the client both verbally AND in writing after every service; the written care instructions must advise the client to consult the body art operator or a qualified health care professional at the first sign of abnormal inflammation/swelling or possible infection. §107(C)(1) — the following information "should be requested" and recorded on the client's written record to promote proper healing: diabetes; history of hemophilia (bleeding); skin diseases, lesions or sensitivities to soap/disinfectants; allergies or adverse reactions to pigments, dyes or other skin sensitivities; epilepsy, seizures, fainting or narcolepsy; pregnancy or breast-feeding/nursing; immune disorders; scarring (keloid). §107(D) — a posted sign (clearly visible, min. letter sizes) with substantially the same risk wording.
- Retention: §107(F) — records kept minimum 3 years. Record contents per §107(C): client name and address; date of each service; description of service; color, manufacturer, and lot number of each pigment used. (Artist/license info on the form/record: UNVERIFIED — not in §107.)
- ID: parent/guardian must show "proper identification" for a minor's procedure (§107(H)(5)). No client photo-ID rule found. UNVERIFIED for the client.
- Barber/cosmetology: none found.
- Citations: La. R.S. 14:93.2; LAC tit. 51, §XXVIII-107(A), (C), (D), (F), (H)(5), (M).

## OK
- Min age tattoo: 18 — outright minor ban. 21 O.S. §842.1(A) — unlawful to tattoo anyone under 18 (parental consent applies only to body piercing: written consent + parent present).
- Parental consent: not allowed for tattoos.
- Required disclosures: OAC 310:233-3-6.1 — consent form must contain: (1) establishment name/address; (2) client name, DOB, address; (3) date; (4) identification/location of procedure(s); (5) artist name and license number; (6) artist signature; (7) client/guardian signature; (8) photocopy of client's government-issued photo ID (or guardian's for a minor piercing); plus health questions — diabetes; hemophilia/excessive bleeding; skin disease, lesions, or sensitivities; allergies/adverse reactions; epilepsy, seizures, fainting, or narcolepsy; anticoagulant medications; pregnant or nursing; when last ate; other pertinent history. OAC 310:233-3-7.1 — verbal AND written aftercare before the procedure begins, including: establishment name/address/phone; artist name; notice that bloodborne disease transmission is still possible; instruction to consult a physician at the first sign of infection; compliance instructions; how to file complaints with the Department.
- Retention: OAC 310:233-3-8 — client records kept confidential, retained 3 years, then destroyed by shredding.
- ID: OAC 310:233-3-6(e)–(f) — acceptable photo ID: driver's license, passport, or state/government ID with name, picture, and DOB (if no DOB on the ID, an original birth certificate must be copied); photocopy retained with the consent form; for a minor piercing: parent/guardian ID + minor's ID incl. original birth certificate; court documents for guardianship.
- Barber/cosmetology: none found.
- Citations: 21 O.S. §842.1(A); OAC 310:233-3-6.1, 310:233-3-6(e)–(f), 310:233-3-7.1, 310:233-3-8. (Sections read from 2021 proposed-rulemaking text; 2022 permanent adoption carries the same sections.)
