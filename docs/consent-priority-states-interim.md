## FLORIDA (fully verified)
- Min age: 16. Under 16 banned except medical/dental by ch. 458/459/466 licensee. — Fla. Stat. § 381.00787(1)
- 16–17 with ALL of: (a) minor accompanied by parent/legal guardian; (b) minor AND parent each produce government-issued photo ID; (c) parent's written NOTARIZED consent on department-prescribed form (DH 4146, 7/12 "Written Notarized Consent For Tattooing of a Minor Child, Age 16 through 17 Years Old"); (d) parent submits proof of parent/guardianship; (e) procedure by licensed tattoo/guest artist or medical/dental licensee. — § 381.00787(2)
- Violation = 2nd-degree misdemeanor. Safe harbor: no violation if artist carefully inspected apparently-valid gov photo ID showing 18+, minor misrepresented self, and a reasonable person would believe it genuine/issued to the minor/truthful. — § 381.00787(3)
- Customer record (per customer, artist completes): artist name; customer name, age, birthdate; tattoo description + body location; customer signature; artist signature; date(s) of procedure. — Fla. Admin. Code 64E-28.009(1)
- 16–17 additional: inspect copies of gov photo ID for minor AND parent (if minor ID lacks birthdate, copy of birth certificate); signed notarized DH 4146 consent; full customer record. — 64E-28.009(3). Gov-issued photo ID = document issued by state, federal, or foreign government with the person's photo. — 64E-28.002(10)
- Aftercare: artist must provide aftercare info to customer verbally AND in writing, before or after the procedure. — 64E-28.009(4)
- Retention: customer records (incl. parental consent) kept 2 years; current licensing period onsite, available for inspection. Personnel records (artist name, address, DOB, license no.) kept 2 years after employment ends. — 64E-28.007(21)(a)–(e)
- Artist license info: active artist license must be displayed publicly at all times; customer record includes artist name. — § 381.00779(2)(a); 64E-28.009(1)(a)
- Establishment must keep each notarized parental consent for period prescribed by department (see 2-yr rule above). — § 381.00779(3)
- Barber/salon: no state-mandated written-consent elements found for chemical services; patch tests not mandated by state rule; straight razors standard for FL barbers, no special consent rule. (LIGHT PASS — no citation found; mark UNVERIFIED if a specific claim is needed.)

## TEXAS (fully verified)
- Min age: 18. Exception ONLY: cover-up of an existing tattoo containing obscene/offensive, gang-related, drug-related, or other markings a parent/guardian/court considers in the minor's best interest to cover — with parental consent. — Tex. Health & Safety Code § 146.012; 25 TAC § 229.406(c)–(d)
- Minor consent mechanics (cover-up only): parent/guardian physically present; executes affidavit of parent/guardian status; presents evidence of minor's identity; presents evidence of own parent/guardian status; supplies studio written detailed description or photograph of tattoo to be covered (kept with permanent records); parent's full name, address, telephone. — 25 TAC § 229.406(d)(2)
- Minor misrepresenting age to get tattoo = Class B misdemeanor. — 25 TAC § 229.406(l)
- ID: at time of tattooing, client presents valid government-issued positive ID (driver's license, passport, military ID, etc.) with photograph AND printed DOB. Artist verifies and documents age, DOB, and ID type in permanent client record. — 25 TAC § 229.406(a)–(b)
- Mandated written disclosures (BEFORE tattoo, verbal AND in writing, to client and parent if applicable): at least — possibility of discomfort/pain; permanence of markings; risk of infection; possibility of allergic reaction to pigments/materials. — 25 TAC § 229.406(h)
- No tattooing anyone appearing under influence of alcohol/drugs; no tattooing skin with unhealthy conditions (rashes, boils, infections, abrasions). — 25 TAC § 229.406(f)–(g)
- Client records (permanent records, kept at studio at least 2 years from date of last entry, available to authorized agent): client name, address, phone; date; client age, DOB, ID type; specific ink color(s) + manufacturer/catalog or ID number when available; body location; artist name; statement that client received a copy of written aftercare instructions and has read and understands them; client signature. — 25 TAC § 229.406(j). Statutory hook: § 146.013 (tattooist keeps permanent record of each person tattooed for board-established period; available for inspection).
- Aftercare: DSHS sample aftercare requires client (and parent if applicable) receive oral AND written aftercare each session (sun avoidance 2 weeks, no swimming, cleanse, antibiotic ointment, sterile bandage, consult practitioner at first sign of infection/allergy, report to artist + DSHS at (512) 834-6711). DSHS sample docs labeled as examples, not mandatory; rule requires the (h)/(j)(7) disclosures + written care instructions receipt statement. — 25 TAC § 229.406(h), (j)(7); DSHS sample aftercare PDF (rev. 6/23/22)
- Barber/salon: no state-mandated written-consent elements found (LIGHT PASS).

## CALIFORNIA (fully verified)
- Min age: 18, NO exceptions for parental consent. Tattooing anyone under 18 = misdemeanor. — Cal. Penal Code § 653 (exception only: licensed healing-arts practitioner in practice). H&S Code § 119302(a): client must be 18 to be offered/receive tattoo or permanent cosmetics, regardless of parental consent.
- Mandated informed-consent form contents — client reads, completes, signs BEFORE procedure; must include at minimum: (1) description of the procedure; (2) what to expect, suggested care, and medical complications that may occur; (3) statement of permanence; (4) NOTICE that tattoo inks/dyes/pigments have NOT been FDA-approved and health consequences are unknown; (5) postprocedure instructions: site care info, activity restrictions (bathing, recreational water, gardening, contact with animals) + durations, infection signs (redness, swelling, tenderness, red streaks toward heart, fever, purulent drainage), when to seek medical care. — H&S Code § 119303(a)
- Mandated questionnaire — client completes and signs BEFORE procedure: possible pregnancy; history of herpes at site, diabetes, latex/antibiotic allergies, hemophilia/other bleeding disorder, cardiac valve disease; medication use incl. prophylactic antibiotics before dental/surgical procedures; other bloodborne-pathogen risk factors. Questionnaire is confidential; no sale/share/transfer; personal medical info handled per HIPAA/state law. — H&S Code § 119303(b)–(c)
- Practitioner registration: 18+, hep B vaccination/immunity/OSHA declination, bloodborne-pathogen training, self-certification; certificate displayed publicly. — H&S Code § 119306(b)–(d)
- Client records retention: state act does not set a duration for general client records; county programs impose it (e.g., CA county temp-tattoo guides require service records kept 2 years: name, address, date of service, ink colors + manufacturer, placement, jewelry details). Mark state-level retention UNVERIFIED; note county-level 2-year practice. Medical info per HIPAA. — H&S § 119303(c)
- Artist/license info on form: CA requires practitioner registration certificate displayed at facility; client records name the practitioner (per county guides: procedure log includes practitioner and client names + date).
- ID rules: no state-prescribed ID-verification procedure in the Act; age verification is the shop's duty (counties inspect "customer 18 or older").
- Barber/salon: no state-mandated written-consent elements found (LIGHT PASS).

## NEW YORK (verified)
- Min age: 18, NO exceptions. Tattooing a child under 18 = unlawfully dealing with a child in the 2nd degree, class B misdemeanor — regardless of parental consent. — N.Y. Penal Law § 260.21(2). NY DOH confirms.
- No state-level consent-form/record statute for tattooing; counties license shops. NYC Health Code (example county rules): § 22-11 — signed written consent per customer listing name, age, DOB, address, telephone, date tattooed, body site; consents maintained 2 years after tattoo date, available for inspection. § 22-12 — written aftercare instructions to prevent infection (sample instructions in DOH publication). § 22-13 — complaint sign (NYC DOH, 125 Worth Street) posted where tattooing is performed. NYC DOH tattoo manual advises informing clients of possible risks, asking about ink/latex allergies, advising pregnant women to consult physician.
- Piercing (not tattoo): PHL Art. 4-A requires written parental consent on DOH form DOH 5072, signed in presence of shop owner/artist for piercing minors; ear piercing exempt.
- Barber/salon: no state-mandated written-consent elements found (LIGHT PASS).

## GEORGIA (verified)
- Min age: 18, NO exceptions for regular tattoo shops. — O.C.G.A. § 16-5-71. Exception: licensed physician/osteopath or technician under direct supervision for medical/cosmetic purposes. DPH Body Art Rules (6)(e): "In accordance with O.C.G.A. Section 16-5-71, no person under the age of eighteen shall be tattooed." O.C.G.A. § 16-12-5: unlawful to tattoo within one inch of the eye socket (misdemeanor).
- Client records: for each client, records of identification, an application, client evaluation, and informed consent form kept in digital or print form, retained minimum 3 YEARS; kept on premises minimum 1 year; all 3 years available to Dept/Health Authority on request; stored prohibiting unauthorized access. — GA DPH Body Art Rules (8), authority O.C.G.A. § 31-40-5
- Aftercare: client has received and understands written AND verbal aftercare. — GA DPH Body Art Rules (7)(d)
- County rules (e.g., North GA Health District, consistent with state rules): client record prepared BEFORE procedure — name + signature, address, proof of age, date, design, location, artist name; statement of informed consent on file; statement by client attesting not under influence of alcohol/drugs on file; signed copy of aftercare procedures on file; signed copy of risk/complication instructions on file.
- Barber/salon: no state-mandated written-consent elements found (LIGHT PASS).

## NORTH CAROLINA (verified)
- Min age: 18, NO exceptions. "It shall be unlawful for any person or persons to tattoo the arm, limb, or any part of the body of any other person under 18 years of age." Parental consent does NOT override (unlike piercing, which requires prior consent of custodial parent). Violation = Class 2 misdemeanor. — N.C.G.S. § 14-400(a)
- Permits: artist permits per G.S. 130A-283 + 15A NCAC 18A .3200 (Section .3200 – Tattooing); permit per artist per location, applied 30 days ahead via county health dept. — 15A NCAC 18A .3202
- Consent-form contents / retention at state level: UNVERIFIED — .3200 rules are sanitation/permit-focused; no state-prescribed consent-form elements or retention period found. (County health departments may add requirements.)
- Barber/salon: no state-mandated written-consent elements found (LIGHT PASS).

## ARIZONA (verified)
- Min age: 18 default; minors tattooed ONLY with physical presence of parent/legal guardian. — A.R.S. § 13-3721(A)(1). Ear-piercing exception: written or verbal permission suffices; health-care-provider-prescribed procedures excepted. — § 13-3721(B)
- ID: requesting age ID and relying in good faith on its accuracy = defense to prosecution. — § 13-3721(C)
- Violation = class 6 felony. — § 13-3721(D)
- No state artist licensure, no state-prescribed consent-form contents, no state retention period; counties/towns set shop guidelines. — AZ SB 1232 fact sheet (2007)
- Barber/salon: no state-mandated written-consent elements found (LIGHT PASS).
